Pulp Fiction follows several interconnected stories in Los Angeles involving hitmen, a boxer, a gangster and his wife, and two small-time robbers. Known for its non-linear storytelling, dark humour, and memorable dialogue, the film explores crime, violence, redemption, and chance as the characters' lives unexpectedly cross paths.
