Tarantino Experience is an immersive exhibition dedicated to the iconic world of Quentin Tarantino. Through his most famous films, visual references, behind-the-scenes facts, and selected short films, visitors will explore the style, storytelling, and cinematic language that have made him one of the most influential directors of contemporary cinema. This experience invites audiences to discover his creative universe in a deeper and more engaging way, making it a unique cultural journey worth living at least once in a lifetime.
